Compliance Task & Onboarding State Machines

Complete transition logic for statutory compliance tasks—including the guarded partner review gate—alongside the client onboarding lifecycle.

01 · Partner Review Guard

The `'In review'` state is strictly guarded: high-risk returns (AFS, ITR14, ITR12, EMP501) and payable IRP6 returns require partner sign-off before submission.

02 · Wait & Signal Handling

Awaiting client documents pauses operational deadlines without losing context, while the `'Missing a date'` signal holds tasks until master profile gaps are resolved.

03 · Lead Onboarding Alignment

The 5-stage onboarding funnel transitions seamlessly into the active compliance rail upon acceptance, immediately generating statutory tasks in `'Not started'`.
