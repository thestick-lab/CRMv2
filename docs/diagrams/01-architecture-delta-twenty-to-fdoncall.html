Architecture Delta · FD on Call Practice Ledger

Twenty CRM to FD on Call Practice CRM, before and after

Transforming the generic open-source Twenty CRM monorepo into FD on Call's specialized practice operating system by pruning generic sales surfaces, integrating the South African statutory compliance engine, adding Documenso digital signing, and linking SharePoint vaults & Xero billing.

01 · Zero UI Re-Invention

By keeping Twenty CRM as our active base, we avoid re-building client registers, task queues, activity timelines, command palettes, and custom databases from scratch.

02 · Configuration Pruning

Generic B2B sales pipelines and ARR customer success features are pruned via Navigation Drawer config and permissions, keeping Twenty clean and focused purely on practice accounting.

03 · Automated Engagement Lifecycle

12-month fee proposals flow directly to self-hosted Documenso for legally binding dual-signature acceptance, auto-activating won leads and creating SharePoint client vaults.
