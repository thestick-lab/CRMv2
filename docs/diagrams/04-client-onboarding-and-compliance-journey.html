Client engagement journey — prospect to first compliance year

Six stages from prospect capture to the first retainer year, with the practitioner's step and the store's response at each. The single dip is conversion: a retainer-only quote carries no statutory scope until services are ticked.

01 · The funnel

Guards hold each transition

Send needs a client and at least one fee line. Sign needs a recorded Documenso envelope id. An active client opens only from Signed.

02 · Conversion

Scope is written, calendar follows

Conversion creates the Client with services from the quoted scope and savedEstimates[code]. The statutory calendar then materialises from those services.

03 · The friction

A retainer-only quote is silent

The build writes no statutory services when the quote carried only retainer lines, so no compliance year appears until the practitioner ticks the missing services.
