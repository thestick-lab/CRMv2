Statutory Compliance & Deadline Engine Logic

Decision tree mapping client metadata (Entity Type, Year-End, VAT Category, Payroll, Provisional status, Incorporation Date) to deterministic statutory SARS and CIPC obligation schedules.

01 · Missing Data Signals

If vital attributes such as incorporation date or financial year-end are missing, the engine emits explicit blocker signals rather than guessing statutory dates.

02 · Entity-Specific Logic

Companies and CCs enforce statutory 6-month AFS and 12-month ITR14 deadlines; trusts use internal 6-month targets; individuals branch by provisional status.

03 · Holiday Overlay & Computus Guard

Every SARS deadline is adjusted to the preceding business day via Section 2(1) Sunday rollover and Easter Computus, overlaying South African gazetted public holidays directly onto the Tasks calendar via core.sa_public_holidays with rollback pills.
