Unknowns are divided into two operational tracks: Track 1 provides copyable research prompts for autonomous agents to verify statutory citations; Track 2 provides interactive sign-off checkboxes for Partners Erik and Wouter.

Track 1: Research-Agent Solvable Prompts

PROMPT 1 · SARS FILING SEASON & IRP6
Verify official gazetted SARS filing season deadlines for 2024, 2025, and 2026 tax years from sars.gov.za. Document exact statutory submission dates for ITR12 (non-provisional and provisional) and ITR12T (Trusts). Verify statutory rules for 3rd provisional tax top-up payments under Paragraph 21 of the Fourth Schedule to the Income Tax Act.
PROMPT 2 · CIPC AR & BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP
Verify official CIPC Annual Return fee tiers for Companies vs Close Corporations under Companies Act s33 and CIPC Regulations. Confirm whether the 30-business-day window starts from anniversary date or month start. Document mandatory Beneficial Ownership filing triggers under General Laws Amendment Act 2022.
PROMPT 3 · XERO SA, SIMPLEPAY & DOCUMENSO
Verify published pricing in ZAR for Xero South Africa (Starter, Standard, Premium excl and incl 15% VAT). Verify SimplePay per-employee pricing tiers and document standard billing formula for payrolls exceeding 50 employees.

05 · Technical Architecture & Database DDL

FD on Call CRM is structured as a TypeScript monorepo using Hono and Drizzle ORM, ensuring identical business logic runs serverless on Cloudflare D1 (Target A) or on a dedicated Node.js + PostgreSQL host (Target B):

apps/web (Frontend)

React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Twenty CRM UX shell (Sidebar, Cmd+K, Table, Kanban, 3-Pane Record Show), and client-side PDF/XLSX generation.

100% Client-Side Reactive

packages/domain (Shared Core)

Pure TypeScript calculation engine lifted directly from Client_CRM. Zero external dependencies; shared between browser and server without drift.

Mathematically Verified

apps/api & packages/db

Hono Web Standards REST/RPC API with Drizzle ORM managing 14 relational tables (including document_attachments and sars_tax_brackets). Deploys to Cloudflare D1 ($0/mo) or Node.js + PostgreSQL.

Dual SQL Dialect Ready
